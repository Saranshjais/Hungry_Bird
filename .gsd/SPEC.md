# SPEC.md — Project Specification

> **Status**: `FINALIZED`

## Vision
To become India's definitive platform for discovering authentic, local street food "Hidden Gems" through community submissions and AI-powered recommendations.

## Goals
1. **Community Discovery**: Allow users to submit and verify local street food vendors.
2. **Intelligent Suggestions**: Provide high-quality recommendations based on cuisine similarity.
3. **Geospatial Navigation**: Enable easy discovery of vendors via interactive maps.

## Non-Goals (Out of Scope)
- Delivery services (Zomato/Swiggy integration) in v1.0.
- Payment gateway integration.

## Users
- **Foodies**: Looking for authentic, non-commercialized street food.
- **Local Guides**: Users who want to share their favorite secret spots.

## Constraints
- **Technical**: Must run efficiently on low-cost hosting (e.g., PythonAnywhere/Render).
- **Data**: Relying on crowdsourced data and Google Places exports.

## Success Criteria
- [ ] 100+ verified vendors across 5 major Indian cities.
- [ ] Working recommendation engine on all city pages.
- [ ] Functional vendor submission pipeline with automated coordinate parsing.
